How Raleigh Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

A little routine care is the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that range and is also showing higher bills, uneven temperatures, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement in peak season.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
