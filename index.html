AC Repair in Chapel Hill

Most air conditioner breakdowns trace back to one failed part: a weak capacitor, a burned contactor, a seized fan motor, or a slow refrigerant leak. We diagnose it with gauges and meters, not guesswork. You get a clear price before any work starts, and if the system is old enough that replacement makes more sense, we'll show you both numbers.

  • Warm air coming from the vents

  • Frozen coil or iced-over lines
  • Water leaking near the indoor unit

Furnace Repair in Chapel Hill

Most furnace breakdowns come from a handful of parts: flame sensors, igniters, inducer motors, and control boards. We find the one that failed and fix it, usually in a single visit. Every visit includes a safety check of the burners and venting, and you'll know the price before we start.

  • No ignition when the heat calls
  • Short cycling on and off
  • Blower running but the air isn't warm
  • New noises when the furnace kicks on

Furnace Won't Stay On
