Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Winston-Salem

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and is also showing rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in Winston-Salem

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. Those are early signs of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Ignored, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
